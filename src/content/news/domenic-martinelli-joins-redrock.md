*Martinelli brings more than a decade of transaction advisory experience as Redrock expands its team serving middle-market founders and family-owned businesses*

SALT LAKE CITY and NEW YORK, Sept. 14, 2026 — Redrock Strategic Advisors, an advisory firm serving middle-market business owners navigating mergers and acquisitions, capital raises, and other critical corporate transactions, today announced that Domenic Martinelli has joined the firm as Director and a member of its founding team. Martinelli expands Redrock’s transaction advisory capabilities and will play a key role in the firm’s development of the next generation of M&A professionals.

“Domenic brings the character, judgment, and transaction experience we want to build Redrock around,” said Geoffrey Loos, Managing Director and Founder of Redrock Strategic Advisors. “He has a strong track record of advising business owners and management teams through important transactions. Combining experienced senior-level advice with thoughtful use of technology, and a highly engaged execution model, is central to how we believe Redrock can deliver exceptional outcomes for our clients.”

Martinelli brings to Redrock more than 10 years of transaction advisory experience across various industries and a range of deal structures. “I’ve built my career helping business owners and management teams navigate critical transactions,” said Martinelli. “That is the work I’m excited to continue at Redrock, alongside a team that shares the same commitment to clients and to building a differentiated advisory firm.”

Martinelli will also play an important role in developing Redrock’s junior professionals and building a defined path from analyst to partner centered on mentorship, increasing responsibility, and hands-on transaction experience.

“I’m passionate about developing talented people and giving them the opportunity to grow into trusted advisors,” Martinelli added. “Redrock’s entrepreneurial and growth-oriented culture made this the right fit.”

Martinelli’s addition represents another step in Redrock’s continued investment in building a senior-led advisory firm designed to serve middle-market business owners through landmark transactions while developing the next generation of M&A professionals.
