Domenic Martinelli is a Director and Co-Founder of Redrock Strategic Advisors.

Domenic has more than 10 years of M&A experience with a focus on advising middle-market companies through M&A transactions including mergers, acquisitions, management buyouts, recapitalizations and carve-outs.

Prior to Redrock, Domenic was a Vice President with CapM Advisors in New York where he completed a broad range of domestic and cross-border transactions and advised clients on mergers and acquisitions and strategic advisory assignments.

Prior to CapM, Domenic worked in PwC’s Deals practice in New York, where he provided transaction advisory services to strategic and private equity clients across a variety of industries.

A Pittsburgh, PA native, Domenic resides on Long Island, NY with his wife and three daughters.
