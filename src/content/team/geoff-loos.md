Geoffrey Loos founded Redrock Strategic Advisors with a two-fold mission of advising middle-market companies on critical corporate transactions while developing the next generation of industry-leading M&A professionals.

Geoff has more than 20 years of investment banking experience with a focus on domestic and cross-border M&A transactions, including mergers, acquisitions, corporate investments, management buyouts, recapitalizations, and carveouts. Mr. Loos's expertise spans key industry sectors including industrial technology, consumer & food, industrial & manufacturing, and business services. Due to his personal background and network, Geoff also covers regional transaction opportunities throughout the Mountain West region.

Prior to founding Redrock, Geoff was a Managing Director with CapM Advisors and a Director with D.A. Davidson's investment banking group, where he was a founding member of the firm's consumer and technology investment banking teams.

Geoff is a Vancouver, B.C.-native and now resides in Utah with his wife and three kids. He actively supports the growth of the local business community as a founding member of a student-run venture fund (University Venture Fund) while attending the University of Utah to more recently participating in local organization.
