Geoffrey Loos founded Redrock Strategic Advisors with a twofold mission: advising middle-market companies on critical corporate transactions while developing the next generation of industry-leading M&A professionals.

Geoff has more than 20 years of investment banking experience with a focus on domestic and cross-border M&A transactions, including mergers, acquisitions, corporate investments, management buyouts, recapitalizations, and carveouts. Mr. Loos's expertise spans key industry sectors including consumer & food, technology, industrial & manufacturing, and business services. Drawing on his background and network, Geoff also advises business owners across industries throughout the Mountain West region.

Prior to founding Redrock, Geoff was a Managing Director with CapM Advisors and a Director with D.A. Davidson's investment banking group, where he was a founding member of the firm's consumer and technology investment banking teams.

A Vancouver, B.C.-native, Geoff now resides in Utah with his wife and three children. He has a long history of supporting the local business community, including as a founding member of the university student-run University Venture Fund and as a board member of the Association for Corporate Growth.
